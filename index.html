FAQ

Important buyer information for memberships and custom requests.

Do I keep premium access forever?

No. Monthly membership access is active while your subscription is active. If the subscription ends, premium roles and future downloads may be removed.

How do I get my Discord role?

After purchase, join the Discord server and open a ticket with your purchase email. Staff will verify your payment and assign the correct role.

Can I share paid files?

No. Premium files and custom request files are for personal use only. Sharing, reuploading, leaking, or reselling paid files is not allowed.

What does the private add-on mean?

The private/non-public add-on means your custom request will not be included in public premium builds. It must be purchased alongside a custom request.

Are these physical products?

No. All products are digital memberships, digital downloads, custom digital content requests, or support access.
